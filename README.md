# **The Maze Runner Trilogy**

My favorite movie series is **The Maze Runner Trilogy**, which is based on the books written by **James Dashner**. It is a science fiction and action series that follows the story of Thomas and a group of teenagers who find themselves trapped in a mysterious maze with no memory of their past.

The trilogy consists of three movies: *The Maze Runner*, *Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials*, and *Maze Runner: The Death Cure*. Throughout the story, Thomas and his friends face different challenges as they try to escape dangerous places, uncover the truth about WCKD, and find a way to survive.

What makes this trilogy my favorite is its exciting story, unexpected plot twists, and the strong friendship between the characters. I also like how it shows the importance of trust, courage, teamwork, and never giving up, even when things become difficult.

The Maze Runner Trilogy is more than just an action movie series to me. It teaches lessons about friendship, sacrifice, and fighting for the people you care about.
